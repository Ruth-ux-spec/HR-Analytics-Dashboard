# HR-Analytics-Dashboard
Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition and workforce trends using HR data. The goal is to identify the key factors influencing employee turnover and provide actionable insights to help improve employee retention.

Tools Used
Power BI
SQL
Python
Dataset
The dataset contains 1,473 employee records, including:
Department
Job Role
Age
Monthly Income
Job Satisfaction
Work-Life Balance
Overtime
Business Travel
Education Field
Attrition Status

Key KPIs
Total Employees: 1,473
Active Employees: 1,236
Employees Left: 237
Attrition Rate: 16.09%
Average Monthly Income: 6,500
Average Age: 37 years

Key Insights
Sales department recorded the highest employee attrition.
Employees with lower salaries showed higher turnover.
Younger employees (18–25 years) left the company more often.
Better job satisfaction was associated with higher employee retention.
Most employees reported good work-life balance.
Overtime and frequent business travel contributed to employee attrition.

Recommendations
Improve employee engagement in the Sales department.
Review compensation for lower-paid employees.
Strengthen career development for younger employees.
Encourage healthy work-life balance.
Monitor overtime and business travel to reduce burnout.
